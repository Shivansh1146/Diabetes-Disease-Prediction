Diabetes Disease Prediction System

Version 1.0.0

Project Overview

This application uses advanced Machine Learning algorithms to predict the likelihood of diabetes in patients based on their clinical diagnostic measurements. It was developed to assist healthcare professionals in early detection and diagnosis.

Technology Stack
  • Backend: Python, Flask, Flask-SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login
  • Machine Learning: Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy (Random Forest / Logistic Regression)
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Glassmorphism UI), JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
  • Database: SQLite
  • Data Visualization: Chart.js
Dataset Information

The model is trained on the Pima Indians Diabetes Dataset. All patients here are females at least 21 years old of Pima Indian heritage. Features include Pregnancies, Glucose, Blood Pressure, Skin Thickness, Insulin, BMI, Diabetes Pedigree Function, and Age.
